# Brag Plan: SmartCam

## What is this app?
A hotel's existing CCTV DVR gets a second pair of eyes: SmartCam watches the cameras
they already own, detects people and vehicles, and writes a plain-English incident
report you can hand to the police.

## The angle
**The cameras were already there. Nobody was watching.** Not 'AI surveillance' - the
mundane, expensive truth that a 32-channel DVR in a hotel lobby records everything and
reports nothing. The product changes nothing about their setup: same cameras, same
recorder, same staff. The claim is narrow and true: it was already looking; now it
tells you.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Nine near-black CCTV tiles blinking on, one per beat, nothing but a low pulse. At
1.9s, small white mono text: **You already own 32 cameras.** At 3.0s, hard cut to
black, four words centred: **Nobody is watching them.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The sentence it writes.** The real description from the app types out over a dim
  frame: 'At 23:34 on Camera 07, a single person is visible, seated at a desk. No
  vehicles or additional people in the immediate vicinity.' Ordinary, specific, and
  unmistakably machine-written - that is the product in one paragraph.
- **The boxes.** A person crossing frame; four corner brackets snap on; a label reads
  person 0.87. Not a highlight reel, instrumentation.
- **The page.** An A4 incident report slides up: camera, time, object, confidence,
  still frame. The artefact that gets a hotel manager to sign, because it is evidence
  that leaves the building.

## Outro / punchline
Three short lines, one per beat: **Your DVR was already looking.** then **Now it tells
you.** then the wordmark and agay.tech. No tagline, no feature list, no button.

## User flow worth showing
Event appears in the dashboard, opens with its written description, Report produces the
PDF. Three beats - and they are the three scenes in the middle.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: control-room restraint, not startup sizzle. Deadpan in the hook,
  warm in the reveal.
- Interpretation: slow, confident cuts on the beat; type that settles and holds; no
  whooshes, no spinning logos, no lens flares. The product is surveillance, so restraint
  reads as competence and anything flashy contradicts the claim.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0a0d13
- Surface: #12161f, hairlines #232a38
- Accent: #4b8cff (brackets, single underline)
- Text: #e8ecf3, muted #98a1b3
- Display font: Inter / system-ui, weight 600, tracking -0.02em
- Body font: ui-monospace for figures, labels, timecodes
- Strongest visual element: the corner-bracket detection overlay on a real frame, and
  the one-page incident report

## Share copy (draft)
Your hotel already owns 32 cameras. Nobody is watching them. SmartCam turns the DVR you
have into written incident reports you can hand to the police.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional bed carrying the pulse, never competing with type
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (109.96 BPM)
- Music treatment: fade in 0-1s low, hold under the hook, lift at the 3.0s reveal,
  settle under the description, ride out from 18.5s, fade by 20.0s
- Music cue guidance: preset cues read; strong beats at 18.01s, 18.55s and 20.19s suit
  the three outro lines; tiles land on the 0.27-1.90 beat grid; the 3.01s cut and the
  9.83s report entrance are both on-beat
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle - bracket snaps and tile entrances only
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched; a soft tick per tile, one low thud on the
  'Nobody is watching them.' cut, a paper slide for the report
- Audio-coupled moments: the typed description (one soft click per line, not literal
  typing sounds), tile entrances, the report rise
- Restraint rule: silence is allowed. The 3.0s cut may hold 200ms of silence before the
  reveal, and that gap is the most effective sound in the video
