## What this is

SmartCam watches the cameras you already have and tells you when something
happens — a person at the gate at an odd hour, someone in a restricted area, a
vehicle that stays too long. Each event arrives as a one-page report you can
print, email, or hand to the police.

It runs on your existing DVR. No new cameras, no cabling, no changes to how your
security staff already work.

## What it does not do

Stated plainly, because a pilot that overpromises is worse than no pilot:

- It does **not** replace your DVR's recording. Your footage stays on your DVR.
- It does **not** work reliably on every camera. Detection needs a reasonable view
  of the area — a camera pointed at a wall, or through a fence, will not perform.
- It carries **up to 8 cameras** on the pilot hardware. More than that needs a
  larger installation.
- Face recognition and person re-identification are **switched off** by default.
  They handle biometric data, which needs a documented lawful basis and your
  written agreement before we would enable them.
- It is **not** a substitute for a security officer. It is a second pair of eyes
  that never looks away.

## What "it works" means

We will agree the specific cameras at installation, then measure for two weeks:

- Does it detect people at the agreed cameras, in both daylight and at night?
- Are the events accurate enough that someone would act on them?
- Do the incident reports look like something you would put in front of a guest or
  the police?

If it fails those, the pilot costs you nothing.

---

## Data and privacy

- Recordings stay on **your** DVR. SmartCam stores events and short clips, not
  continuous video.
- Events are held for 30 days by default, configurable.
- Video is processed on equipment on your network and on a server we operate;
  it is not shared with third parties.
- Face recognition and re-identification are disabled unless you ask for them in
  writing.
- Nigerian data protection (NDPA) requires signage telling people cameras are
  operating. We will provide the wording for your entrances.
